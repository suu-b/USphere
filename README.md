# USphere

USphere is aimed to become a personal, self-curating video discovery and recommendation system.  

USphere sources its videos from YouTube via its data API. I want to dispense any shallow misrepresentation of USphere as a custom youtube implementation or just a recommendation algorithm. It is rather a higher abstraction that maintains and evolves its own model of discovery and recommendation. 

There are three core functions:  

## Discovery
Discovery is the function to expand the model's pool of YouTube knowledge. 
Channel Pooling is one mechanism where starting from seeded channels, the system will traverse their collaborations to build the pool of channels in a structure I have termed the 'Sphere.

See Sphere for more.

### Other mechanisms
There are other mechanisms as well - Video Pooling (see [MUSING.md](./MUSING.md)) and shortcomings of the current channel pooling model as well. I have talked about them rather deeply here. Out of the apparent solutions, controlled exploration is a serious consideration. It will inject new channels in the crust and if they gain engagement they will rise. But I am a bit reticient to add it. For now, I want to focus on building Channel Pooling.

## Recommendation 

Recommendation acts on the pool of discovered pool of channels or videos. The job is to select content for a viewing session. The channel's position in the sphere will influence its recommendation to a lot extent. I may need to come up with a nice scheme to balance novelty and the sphere.

## Consumption

Consumption produces the feedback on the curated content that the user has been served. It will capture user's interaction with the videos, derive insights from there, score videos and the candidates based on that and promote and demote them. 
This is the layer that will make the sphere move from one state to another. Moreover, the recommendation layer will adjust its scheme based on metrics generated by the consumption layer.


# Sphere

One can judge, that the sphere is the primary component of the system.

The sphere will be a three-layered index of channels metadata - Core, Mantle, and Crust. 
The layers represent a hierarchy of relevance. 
Initially, the core will be populated by explicitly seeded channels, mantle - channels discovered through the relationships with core, and crust - channels discovered through the relationships with the mantle.

However, the sphere will evolve with time. Channels will be promoted and demoted based on their relevance. **This point accomodates the fact in the system that my interests will evolve and that even a core channel may lose its ranking**. But this risks the crust to become a graveyard for dead interests. To resolve this - we would have an archive. You can take it like an underworld of some sort. 
Channels demoted from even the crust will be here. We are not completely ousting them from the system because interests revise.

Thus, the sphere works on the principle of reward and punishment. 
Every channel is given opportunity to demonstrate its value before demoting it. Sustained evidence of irrelevance leads to demotion the status is not preserved indefinitely.
